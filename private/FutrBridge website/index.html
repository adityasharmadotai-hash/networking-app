Why both

Research and recruiting are the same problem.

You cannot judge an AI researcher without understanding the research. Most firms pick one side: labs that do the work, or agencies that sell the people. FutrBridge does both on purpose — the research practice keeps us honest about what great looks like, and the talent practice keeps us connected to everyone doing it.

  • Practitioners, not intermediaries. The people screening your candidates are the same people running our evaluations.
  • Depth over volume. Short, defensible shortlists with written technical rationale — not a spray of profiles.
  • Bay Area rooted. Based where the frontier labs, the compute, and the capital are — with a network that reaches well past them.
  • Measured, then shipped. Every engagement ends in something you can run, audit, and hand to your team.

How we work

A short path from problem to proof.

Whether the mandate is research or hiring, the shape is the same: understand the real constraint, do the work in the open, hand back something measurable.

01

Scope

A working session to define the actual problem — the model behaviour that is failing, or the role nobody else has been able to fill.

02

Map

We build the landscape: the relevant literature and baselines, or the real market of people who can do this job today.

03

Engage

Experiments run, or conversations start. You see the working notes as they happen — no month-long silence.

04

Deliver

A result you can act on: a reproducible finding with code, or a shortlist with written technical rationale per candidate.
