1. Introduction

FutrBridge LLC ("FutrBridge", "we", "us" or "our") is an AI research and AI talent firm based in San Francisco, California. This policy explains what personal information we collect through futrbridge.com and in the course of our research and recruiting services, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number, employer and role, when you email us or complete a form.
  • Candidate information — résumé or CV, work history, education, publications and code, skills, location and work authorization status, compensation expectations, references, and interview feedback.
  • Client information — role requirements, job descriptions, hiring-process details, and business contact details for your team.
  • Correspondence — the content of messages you send us.

Information we collect automatically

  • Usage and device data — IP address, browser type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed and timestamps, collected through server logs and any analytics we use.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — see section 7.

Information from other sources

For our talent practice we may collect professional information from publicly available and third-party sources, including professional networks such as LinkedIn, public code repositories, academic publications, conference listings, company websites and referrals from people in our network.

3. How we use information

  • to respond to enquiries and provide our research and recruiting services;
  • to identify, assess, contact and represent candidates for specific roles;
  • to present candidate profiles to clients, with the candidate's knowledge;
  • to manage engagements, contracts, invoicing and records;
  • to send service updates and, where permitted, occasional relevant opportunities or market information — you can opt out at any time;
  • to operate, secure, analyse and improve the site;
  • to comply with legal obligations and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK visitors)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: legitimate interests (operating our business, professional recruiting outreach, site security); consent (certain marketing and non-essential cookies — withdrawable at any time); contract (taking steps at your request and performing our agreements); and legal obligation where applicable.

5. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • With clients — candidate profiles and assessments are shared with a prospective employer only with the candidate's permission for that specific role.
  • With service providers — email, applicant-tracking, CRM, cloud hosting, analytics and scheduling vendors that process data on our instructions.
  • For legal reasons — where required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety and the integrity of our services.
  • In a business transaction — in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

6. International transfers

We are based in the United States and our service providers may process data in the United States and elsewhere. Where we transfer personal information out of the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we use an appropriate safeguard such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Cookies and analytics

The site uses cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary for it to function, and may use analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies; doing so may affect parts of the site. Where required by law, we ask for consent before setting non-essential cookies.

8. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here. Candidate records are typically retained for up to 24 months after our last meaningful contact so that we can consider you for future roles, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or a longer period is required by law. Client and contractual records are retained for the life of the engagement plus any applicable legal or tax retention period.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port, restrict or object to our processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what we collect, to request deletion or correction, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

To exercise a right, email privacy@futrbridge.com. We will verify your request and respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You may also ask us at any time to stop contacting you about opportunities and to remove you from our candidate database.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including access controls, encryption in transit and vendor diligence. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

The site and our services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Third-party sites

Our site links to third-party services such as LinkedIn and YouTube. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version, and material changes will be highlighted on this page.

14. Contact us

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@futrbridge.com
