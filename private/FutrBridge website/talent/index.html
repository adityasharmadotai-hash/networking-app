The people who can actually build it.

Who we place

Roles where the bar is technical, not administrative.

From seed-stage teams hiring their first research engineer to scale-ups building out a platform org.

ROLE / 01

Research scientists & engineers

Pre-training and post-training, RL, alignment and evaluation. People whose work you can read, and who can explain why it worked.

ROLE / 02

ML & applied AI engineers

The engineers who get models into products: agents, retrieval, fine-tuning pipelines, latency and cost, and the evaluation loop around it.

ROLE / 03

AI infrastructure & platform

Training and inference infrastructure, GPU and cluster work, data platforms, and the serving layer that keeps it all standing up.

ROLE / 04

Leadership & founding teams

Heads of AI, founding engineers, research leads and technical product owners — hires where a mistake costs a year.

The process

From kickoff to signed offer.

You get a named partner throughout — the same person who scoped the role reads every profile that reaches you.

01

Calibrate

We pull apart the role with your hiring manager: the real problems this person solves, the must-haves, and the trade-offs you would accept.

02

Map the market

A written map of who exists, where they are, what they cost, and how your offer reads against the alternatives they already have.

03

Engage & assess

Direct, specific outreach, then a technical conversation. Every shortlisted candidate arrives with written rationale, not a résumé summary.

04

Close

We stay in the loop through interviews, references, compensation and the counter-offer conversation, until the start date holds.
