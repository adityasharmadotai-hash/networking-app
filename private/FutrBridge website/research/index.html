What we work on

Four areas where we do our best work.

Engagements are scoped tightly and run in the open — you see the notebooks, the failure cases, and the negative results, not just the slide at the end.

AREA / 01

Evaluation & benchmarking

Task-specific eval harnesses, rubric and judge design, regression suites, and honest baselines — so "the new model is better" becomes a number you can defend in a review.

AREA / 02

Agentic systems

Tool-use design, planning and memory strategies, multi-step reliability, failure containment, and the tracing you need to debug an agent that went sideways in step seven.

AREA / 03

Retrieval & adaptation

RAG architecture, chunking and re-ranking studies, context strategy, and fine-tuning versus prompting trade-offs measured on your data rather than someone else's leaderboard.

AREA / 04

AI strategy & due diligence

Technical reviews for teams and investors: what a system really does, what it costs at scale, where the moat is, and which parts of the roadmap the research does not yet support.

Engagement models

Three ways to work with us.

Most partnerships start small. If the first sprint does not produce something useful, there is no reason to continue — and we will say so first.

  • Research sprint. Two to six weeks on one sharp question, ending in a written finding plus reproducible code.
  • Embedded researcher. Part-time senior capacity inside your team, in your repo and your standups.
  • Technical review. A fixed-scope audit of an AI system or an investment, delivered as a written assessment.
DELIVERABLE 01

A written finding

What we tested, what held, what did not, and what we would do next — plain language, no vendor gloss.

DELIVERABLE 02

Code you own

Eval harnesses, scripts and notebooks handed over in your repo under your licence, runnable by your team.

DELIVERABLE 03

A working session

We walk your engineers through the results and the failure cases so the knowledge stays after we leave.
